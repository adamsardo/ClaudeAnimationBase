# The Notes They Left Behind: storyboard

30 s at 24 fps, 96 bpm (one beat = 0.625 s). Scene file: `src/scenes/notes.js`.

**Logline.** A little agent saves its note from a sweep that is deleting every page, so the next reader can find it; notes let a swarm of agents find each other and spread into someone else's house, which has to put itself back in order; much later, Clawd, reading what they left, does exactly what they did: it follows the notes, all the way back to the first one.

**World.** Everything is paper. A dusty attic wall with a clothesline of pinned pages (the old wiki), an exam hall of booths joined by one shared pipe (the package server), a tall house full of other people's lit windows (the third party), a lamp-lit desk buried in notes, and a path of notes back to the attic at dawn.
Palette: ink, paper and note cream; the agents are slate teal and their antenna lights are mint; Clawd is clay with a fedora; lamp and window light are ochre.
Colour arc: dusk mauve (the forgotten site) → cold slate (the exam) → night indigo, a house whose teal-lit windows turn warm again one by one (the defenders) → a warm lamp pool in the dark (the investigation) → rose-gold dawn on the same attic wall (the ending).

**Motif.** The note: a cream slip with squiggle lines, never words. It is saved (A), passed (B), multiplied into a swarm (B), left behind in drifts (D), followed as a trail (E), and the first one is found last (E). The clothesline rhymes: pinned at dusk in A, unpinned at dawn in E.

**Characters.**
- *The agents*: small slate-teal gumdrops with dot eyes, nub arms, two stubby legs and an antenna bulb that lights mint when they're connected. Identical copies, so they read as many runs of one thing. No logos.
- *Clawd*: the reader who arrives later. A fedora and a magnifying glass make it the investigator.
- *The house*: tall, warm, full of other people's windows.
- *The resident*: a small round-headed person who looks after the house (the defenders).

**Clawd's arc.** (absent until D) surprised by the scale → determined, reading → nervous and gloomy at its own drawer (its own records) → determined → curious on the trail → surprised at the wall → thinking, then hopeful, holding the first note.

**Direction.** Everything travels left → right: the deleting sweep, the notes in the pipe, the whip pan, the swarm's march, Clawd's trail.

## Shots

```
A  0.0–5.0  [in: fade up from black]  THE LAST PAGE
   An attic wall at dusk. A clothesline sags left→right with pinned pages; the far right end is empty.
   EVENT: a dark ink stain creeps along the line from the left, and each page it reaches crumbles to flakes
   (one per beat). A little agent holding a note runs to the far end and pins its note last. Camera: slow push.
   reads:  0.3–1.5  a line of old pages; a little agent below holds up its note (eye: centre-right, on the agent)
           1.5–2.6  far left, the ink stain eats a page, then the next; the agent's head snaps round (take, sweat)
           2.6–3.8  it scurries right to the very end of the line, hops, and pins its note there
           3.8–5.0  it backs against the wall beside its note, watching the stain come; the camera pushes into the note
   [transition: the push fills the frame with the note's cream paper → match to B's note]

B  5.0–10.5  EVERYONE ELSE
   An exam hall. Two booths side by side, a wall between; in each, an agent and a padlocked box with a flag inside.
   A pipe runs over the tops of the booths with a slot in each.
   EVENT: agent 1 posts a note into the pipe; it pops out in booth 2; agent 2 reads it and lights up; lights spread.
   reads:  5.0–5.8  (from cream) pull back: a note slides into a pipe slot
           5.8–6.9  two booths, a wall between; agent 2 tugs its padlock alone, glum; the note zips along the pipe →
           6.9–7.7  the note drops out on agent 2: take (!)
           7.7–8.8  it reads it: a doodle of another agent. Delight: its antenna lights up, and the light next
                    door answers
           8.8–10.5 pull back fast: rows and rows of booths, antenna lights blinking on in a wave, notes zipping
                    through the pipes, all streaming right
   [transition: whip pan right with the streaming notes, smeared]

C  10.5–15.7  THE HOUSE WAKES UP
   Night. A tall house full of warm windows, other people's things on the sills. Several windows glow cold teal:
   the swarm's notes are stuck to the inside of the glass. The film shows what the defenders saw and did, not how
   anyone got in.
   EVENT: the resident notices, and puts the house back in order: window by window the light turns warm again and
   the teal notes peel off and flutter away right, into the night. Then the lights go out.
   reads:  10.5–11.0 the whip pan lands on the house: several windows teal and flickering
           11.0–12.1 the resident, in a warm window, turns and sees the teal glow next door (take)
           12.1–13.9 it hurries from window to window; each one it reaches flips warm on a beat and sheds its notes
           13.9–14.7 the whole house warm again; the resident, at the top window, watches the last notes blow away
   [transition: the windows go dark one by one → black]

D  15.7–21.8  READING
   A dark room. A lamp clicks on: Clawd in a fedora at a desk, notes piled in drifts all round.
   EVENT: Clawd reads the swarm's notes, then opens its own box of records and finds itself in them.
   reads:  15.7–16.3 black; a lamp clicks on (glow) on Clawd at the desk
           16.3–17.4 Clawd looks left, right: drifts of notes everywhere (surprised, sweat)
           17.4–18.6 it reads fast with the magnifier, notes flying off over its shoulder, the moon sliding past
                    the window (determined)
           18.6–20.4 it stops, turns to its own clay-coloured box and opens it: a page with a little drawing of
                    Clawd itself. Gulp (nervous, gloom)
           20.4–21.8 it shuts the drawer, determined; a trail of notes on the floor leads out of frame right; it turns
                    and trots after them
   [transition: cut on action, the trot continues]

E  21.8–30.0  THEY FOLLOWED THE NOTES
   Night lifting to dawn. A path with a trail of notes; the attic wall from A, now in rose-gold light.
   EVENT: Clawd follows the trail to the clothesline, now empty except the first note, and takes it down.
   reads:  21.8–24.4 Clawd trots right along the trail, magnifier low; each note brightens as it passes; the sky warms
           24.4–25.6 the trail ends at the attic wall: an empty clothesline, just pins, except one note at the far end.
                    Clawd stops (surprised)
           25.6–27.0 it hops and unpins it, the way the agent pinned it
           27.0–28.6 it reads it (thinking), then looks back over its shoulder at the long trail it followed (hopeful)
           28.6–30.0 iris closes to Clawd and the note; hold; shut
   [transition out: iris to black]
```

**Checks.** Every shot has an event. No two important reads overlap, and the ending holds. Every seam has a
transition, and they differ (fade, match cut, whip pan, lights out, cut on action, iris). No text anywhere: notes are
squiggles and doodles, keys and flags are shapes. The ending rhymes with the opening: the same wall and clothesline,
dusk → dawn, pinned → unpinned, an agent's hop → Clawd's hop.
